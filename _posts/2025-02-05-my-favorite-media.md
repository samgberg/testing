*Last updated: February 2025*

## Podcasts

1. **[The Startup Solution With Heidi Roizen](https://www.threshold.vc/podcast)** (Heidi Roizen, Threshold Ventures): A venture capital and entrepreneurship podcast offering guidance and solutions on some of the most pressing topics faced by entrepreneurs from writing term sheets to managing difficult board members to leading a relationship-driven life, The Startup Solution is a must listen! Heidi Roizen beautifully combines her knowledge from 40 years in the tech startup world with her unmatched wit and personability. I have been an avid fan since the first episode!

2. **[The Headlines with Tracy Mumford](https://www.nytimes.com/column/the-headlines)** (The New York Times): A perfect listen with your morning coffee (or oat milk latte if you're like me!) A quick 10 minute daily briefing (M-F) on the latest news and events. A great way to stay caught up on recent events.

## Books

1. **[Designing Your Life](https://designingyour.life/)** (Bill Burnett and Dave Evans, 2016): Utilizing design thinking methods to navigate the complex intricacies of designing a well lived life, Designing Your Life is the perfect read for anyone looking to improve their life through thought provoking exercises while feeling like you are sitting in a class at Stanford's d.school! This book is perfect for anyone who loves a worksheet and creative exercise. I would highly recommend listening to the audiobook version as Bill Burnett narrates us through our life design journeys.

## Journals

1. **[Journal of Product Innovation Management](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/15405885)**: A peer-reviewed journal focusing on managerial knowledge of innovation management and product development. I have been reading articles from here for my Trinity College Dublin New Product Development module and found many great articles! Check out my [review of a JPIM paper on drug development](https://samgberg.github.io/testing/2025/failure-and-success-in-drug-development/).

## Blogs and Insights

1. **[Scientific, Technical, and Industry White Papers](https://www.levervc.com/insights/)** (Lever VC): A great insights page on exciting news and technologies on the future of food. With Lever VC's combination of financial and scientific expertise, this page has great insights and provides a lens into the future of food space!
