I'm Sam Goldberg, a student and researcher who loves the place where science meets business. Most of my work is in **cellular agriculture**: growing real meat from animal cells, without raising and slaughtering animals.

## What I've worked on

- **Tufts Center for Cellular Agriculture** (Tufts University): research since my freshman year on cell line development, sensory analysis, serum-free media, consumer acceptance, and economic analysis.
- **UPSIDE Foods**, **Asentia Foods**, and the **TUCCA Commercialization Lab**: hands-on work at alt-protein and cultivated meat companies.
- **Trinity College Dublin**: coursework in new product development.

## What I write about

Biotech, food tech, and marketing. Some posts explain a technology from the ground up. Others are short takes on a research paper or business article and what it means for new industries.
